About Jajeh Financial

Jajeh Financial was founded in 2014 to help businesses and owners in critical seasons in the life cycle of the company. Since then, the firm has grown to focus on fractional CFO work, specializing in the season of transition, primarily exiting.

Patrick K Currie has been in the accounting & business world for over 20 years. Having managed over $150 million in client assets, his expertise is built on experience and a well-proven team of professionals necessary for complex transactions in the small to middle market segment.
